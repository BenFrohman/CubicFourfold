## Theorem (Zucker, 1977)

The Hodge conjecture holds for every smooth cubic fourfold \(X \subset \mathbb{P}^5\).
Every class in \(H^4(X,\mathbb{Q})\cap H^{2,2}(X)\) is algebraic.

## Scope

This is a third subject next to NL (\(d\ge 6\)) and Fermat planes.
It is not a proof of Hodge on a general fourfold.
